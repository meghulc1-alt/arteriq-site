The science

What the research really says.

We tell you what studies show, and what they don’t. No hype, no big promises. That’s why every Protocol 60 starts and ends with a test of your own blood.

Based on published research available up to 2024

The ingredient

Meet nattokinase.

Nattokinase is a natural enzyme. It is made when soybeans are fermented to make natto, a traditional Japanese food.

From natto

Made by fermenting soybeans with a friendly bacteria, Bacillus subtilis.

Measured in FU

Its strength is measured in fibrinolytic units (FU), not milligrams. FU shows how active the enzyme is.

10,000 FU

Each Arter.IQ sachet has 10,000 FU. Stir it into water and take it on an empty stomach.

We left vitamin K2 out on purpose, so it does not interfere with blood thinners such as warfarin. Arter.IQ is for adults aged 35 and over. It is a supplement, not a medicine.

How it works

Three ways it may help.

1

Breaks down fibrin

Fibrin is the protein mesh that holds a blood clot together. Nattokinase can break it down directly.

2

Supports your body’s own clot clearing

It may boost the natural system your body already uses to clear small clots.

3

May ease blood pressure a little

It may calm an enzyme (ACE) that tightens blood vessels. This fits the small blood pressure drops seen in trials.

It works slowly and gently. It is far weaker than prescription clot-busting medicines, and it is not a substitute for them.

The evidence

What trials found, rated honestly.

These ratings come from placebo-controlled trials and reviews of them. Studies without a placebo group, or with authors linked to a manufacturer, are noted separately.

Modest benefit

Blood pressure

−3 / −2 mmHg

Top and bottom numbers fell by about 3 and 2 points more than with a placebo, in 3 short trials of about 8 weeks (2,000 to 6,000 FU a day).

Li et al., Rev Cardiovasc Med, 2023

Mild effect

Blood clotting

Still normal

Clotting tests (PT and aPTT) ran slightly longer than with a placebo, but stayed within the normal range. This matters for anyone on blood thinners.

Yoo et al., Food Funct, 2019

No clear benefit

Cholesterol

Not shown

Combined controlled trials found no clear improvement in any cholesterol measure. One study in China reported a benefit, but its authors had conflicts of interest.

Li et al., 2023; Chen et al., 2022

Not shown in trials

Artery walls

No change

A 3-year trial of 265 adults (2,000 FU a day) found no change in artery wall thickness compared with a placebo. A manufacturer-linked study at 10,800 FU reported thinner walls.

Hodis et al., 2021 (NAPTS); Chen et al., 2022

Safety first

Who should not take it.

Do not use without specialist supervision if you

  • Take blood thinners: warfarin, heparin, LMWH or DOACs
  • Take antiplatelet medicines: aspirin, clopidogrel, ticagrelor or prasugrel
  • Have a bleeding disorder, or have had a brain bleed
  • Have an active stomach or gut bleed, or an ulcer
  • Have very low or uncontrolled low blood pressure
  • Are allergic to soy
  • Are pregnant or breastfeeding
  • Are under 18

Before surgery or dental work

Stop at least 14 days before any planned surgery, dental procedure or other invasive procedure. Tell your surgeon, anaesthetist and dentist that you take nattokinase.

Side effects

Uncommon, usually mild: stomach upset, headache, skin rash.
Rare: allergic reactions, mainly in people sensitive to soy. Serious bleeding has been reported in rare case reports, including with aspirin.

Stop and get urgent help if you notice

Unusual bruising, bleeding that won’t stop, black or tarry stools, a severe headache, swelling of the face, or difficulty breathing.

Medicines and supplements

Tell your doctor about everything you take.

These can add to the risk of bleeding when taken with nattokinase.

Our promises

What we will always tell you.

1

Supplement, never substitute

Never stop or replace statins, blood pressure medicines, blood thinners or any prescribed treatment for Arter.IQ.

2

No cholesterol claims

Controlled trials have not shown a meaningful drop in LDL cholesterol. Arter.IQ is not a statin.

3

You may not feel it

Blood pressure changes in trials were small and took about 8 weeks. Measurements matter, not how you feel. That’s why we test.

4

The dose is honest

Our 10,000 FU dose is above the tested range. Benefits at this dose are estimated, not yet proven.

5

Bleeding risk is real, if rare

That’s why every Protocol 60 starts with a safety check before your first sachet.

6

Tell every doctor

Mention nattokinase to every doctor, pharmacist, dentist and surgeon you see.

7

Never for emergencies

Nattokinase is not a clot-busting treatment. For a suspected heart attack, stroke or clot, call 112 straight away.

8

Your results are yours

A change between your two tests shows what happened over 60 days. It can’t prove nattokinase alone caused it, so we review it with you honestly.

9

Who should ask first

Avoid it without specialist advice if you have a bleeding disorder or soy allergy. Ask first if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, as safety information is limited.
