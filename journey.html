Your 60 days

What happens after you start.

From your first blood test to your answer, step by step.

i.Before day 1

Safety check

A quick WhatsApp check makes sure Arter.IQ is right for you and the medicines you take.

ii.Day 0

First blood test

Someone comes to your home to take a small blood sample, and you note your blood pressure. This is your starting point.

iii.Days 1 to 60

One sachet a day

Stir one into water every morning and keep taking your usual medicines. WhatsApp reminders help you stay on track.

iv.Days 56 to 60

Second blood test

The same blood test and blood pressure check again, at the same lab, so the numbers are fair to compare.

v.After day 60

Your answer

A specialist goes through both reports with you, side by side, in plain words.
